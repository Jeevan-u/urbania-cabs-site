                      Questions

                      Frequently asked questions

                      A few practical answers before you send your enquiry.

                      How do I make a booking with Urbania Cabs Bangalore?
                      Choose a trip type in the enquiry form and share your route, dates, passenger count and contact details. Your details open in WhatsApp and are sent to our team. We then confirm availability and pricing with you directly.
                      Is this an online payment website?
                      No. This is an enquiry-based website. No payment is collected online and no instant booking is confirmed by the website. The trip is arranged after our team confirms the details with you.
                      Which vehicles can I request?
                      We can arrange sedans, SUVs, Innova vehicles, Force Urbania vans, Tempo Travellers and buses. Availability, seating layout and pricing are confirmed with each enquiry.
                      Can you handle group travel and events?
                      Yes. Share your headcount, luggage, itinerary and event timings. We will suggest Urbania, Tempo Traveller or bus options based on the group and route.
                      Do you cover outstation trips from Bangalore?
                      Yes. We take one-way and round-trip outstation enquiries from Bangalore. Enter your pickup point, destination, dates and return date if applicable.
                      How will I know my enquiry was received?
                      WhatsApp opens with a formatted copy of your enquiry. Press send there to deliver it to our team. If automatic team notification is configured, the website will also confirm that on screen after you submit.
                      Can I use my current location for pickup?
                      Yes. Use the location button next to the pickup field and allow browser access. If permission is denied or address lookup is unavailable, you can type the address manually.
                      Are fares shown on the website?
                      No. Fares depend on the vehicle, distance, duration and trip type. We confirm pricing directly after reviewing your enquiry. There are no prices or payment steps on this website.
                      How is my enquiry data handled?
                      Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry. Read the privacy notice for the full explanation of how data is handled and who processes it.
