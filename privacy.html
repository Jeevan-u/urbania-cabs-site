Privacy notice

This notice explains what we collect when you send a booking enquiry, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who we are

Urbania Cabs Bangalore is an enquiry-based cab rental service operating from Bangalore, Karnataka, India. For privacy questions or requests, contact us on +91 96323 45415 or at skycabsbangalore@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form, we collect the details you enter. Depending on the trip type, this can include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Company name and contact person for corporate enquiries.
  • Pickup, drop and destination addresses, dates, times, passenger count, luggage details, vehicle preference and any notes you add.
  • A reference number, the page you sent the enquiry from, and the time of submission.
  • Basic technical information such as your IP address, used only for spam prevention and rate limiting.

If you press the current-location button and allow the browser permission, we receive your approximate coordinates to help fill the pickup or drop field. You can type an address manually instead, and the enquiry still works.

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank details or any other payment information on this website. This is an enquiry-only service.

How we use your details

  • To respond to your enquiry and confirm vehicle availability and pricing.
  • To arrange the journey with the driver or operating partner assigned to your trip.
  • To keep a record of the enquiry and our reply for customer service and accounting purposes.
  • To prevent spam, abuse and duplicate submissions.
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations that apply to us.

We rely on your consent when you submit the form, and on our legitimate business interest in responding to enquiries and arranging transport. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, although we may need to keep records where the law requires it.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed for the purpose above:

  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you press send in WhatsApp, your message is delivered through WhatsApp under its own privacy terms.
  • Telegram: if our team notification service is configured, the enquiry details are sent to a private Telegram chat used by our team.
  • Google Places: if address suggestions are enabled, the text you type in an address field is sent to Google Places to return suggestions. The lookup is made through our server so the browser does not receive our API key.
  • Hosting provider: our website and serverless functions are hosted on Vercel, which processes standard request logs.
  • Drivers and operating partners: the pickup, drop, date, time and contact details needed to operate your confirmed journey.

We do not sell your personal details. We do not use them for third-party advertising.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The site may use your browser's session storage to remember that an enquiry was just sent, so a refresh does not create a duplicate. Your browser may store form values or permissions according to its own settings.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to respond, operate the journey and meet legal, tax or accounting obligations. When those purposes are complete, we delete or anonymise the details. Short-lived spam and rate-limit data is held in server memory and expires automatically.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law in India, you can ask us to:

  • Confirm what personal details we hold about you.
  • Correct details that are wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete details we no longer need.
  • Withdraw consent for future contact.
  • Raise a complaint about how we handled your details.

Send requests to skycabsbangalore@gmail.com or call +91 96323 45415. We may ask for proof of identity before acting on a request so that we do not disclose details to the wrong person.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging travel. If a journey is for a child, the enquiry should be made by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect details directly from children.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services or legal obligations change. The latest version is always published on this page with the date shown above.

This notice is a clear summary of our current practice. It is not legal advice. If you need a formal data-processing agreement for corporate travel, contact us and we will discuss it before the journey is confirmed.
